Recording of the installation

Current’s Wanderers was active for sixteen days, from June 11 to June 26, 2026, at the ZHdK diploma Exhibition. During that period of time, it enabled a real-time physical interaction with plankton life in Lake Greifensee. This might have been one of the first attempts to bridge the perceptual gap between planktonic and human ecosystems through a real-time multisensory interface.
